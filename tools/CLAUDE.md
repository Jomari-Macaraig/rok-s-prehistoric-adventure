# tools/: asset pipeline

`prepare_assets.py` (needs Pillow) reads `../Assets` without changing it and writes `assets/`:
- `bg/bg_1..3.png`: the layers resized to 1280×720. bg_2 (mountains) is also cropped horizontally to its art (1138 px wide) so it can tile without gaps. bg-4.png is no longer used.
- `terrain/*.png` + `terrain/terrain.json`: **now written by `prepare_terrain.py` from the user's seamless pack** (see below); `terrain()` here is the old path: ground pieces cropped from the Terrain Tiles pack and scaled ×0.55. They are mid_a and mid_b (tiles 02/03), cap_left and cap_right (01/04), slope_up and slope_down (Terrain-variation_01/02), drop (tile 10, scaled so its drop equals one level) and dirt (Seamless-tileable-textures_02). Crops trim the outline *and* the darker shading just inside it on every edge that joins a neighbour; outer edges keep their outline. The JSON stores each piece's size, its grass-tip line at the left, middle and right, a smoothed grass `profile` used for physics, and `level_step`, the slope's rise (47 px).
- `characters/<form>/<anim>.png`: the Rok pack sheets scaled by 7/16 with Lanczos. idle, walk, run and jump come from `Rok_Complete_Animation_Pack`; duck and slide come from `Rok_Duck_Slide_Addon` (same canvas sizes and pivots). All 10 Pteranodon sheets (idle, walk, run, jump, duck, slide, fly, glide, takeoff, land) come from `Pteranodon_Animations/pteranodon`; its 512×512 canvas becomes 224×224 with pivot (112, 213.5).
- Zones (`zones()`): `bg/desert_far.png` is the mesa backdrop with its baked-in sky keyed out (so the game's day/night sky shows through); `bg/desert_dunes.png` is the dunes. `terrain/desert_ground.png` is the sandstone ground strip (rows 766+) with its body repeated and cross-faded below to 420 px. The volcanic set (`bg/volcanic_far.png` with its ash sky keyed out, `bg/volcanic_dunes.png` from the mid layer, `terrain/volcanic_ground.png`) comes from the supplied `Rok_Trap_Expansion/volcanic_biome` art. `terrain/cobble.png` is cropped from Seamless-tileable-textures_03 and is currently unused.
- `characters/<form>/die.png` + `characters/die.json`: the five death-hop sheets in `Assets/Rok` (4×2 grids with non-integer cells, 443.5 px). `deaths()` cuts each cell, trims it, scales the form so frame 0 matches its in-game standing height (Rok 168, Raptor 112, Triceratops 136, Spinosaurus 152, Pteranodon 124 px), and packs the frames on a shared canvas aligned bottom-centre. The JSON gives each form's frame size and feet pivot.
- `characters/trex/*.png` + `portraits/trex.png`: the T-rex pursuer sheets (idle, run, roar, bite) from `Trex_Chaser_Animations/Rok_Enemies/trex`, scaled ×0.875 to a 672×448 frame with pivot (336, 427).
- `portraits/<form>.png`: HUD button icons. Each is the first idle frame of that form, trimmed. The large character renders in `../Assets/Rok` have opaque white backgrounds, so they are not used.
- `props/*.png`: environment props trimmed to their alpha bounds and scaled ×0.45. The texture size is the visual size used for hitboxes.
- `wind/<name>.png` + `wind/wind.json`: the vegetation wind sheets scaled ×0.5 with frame size, root pivot and frame time.
- `powerups/<art>_idle.png` + `powerups/<art>.png` (`powerups()`): the power-up art from `Rok_Powerups`: the 128 px 4×2 idle loop (`animations/128/<id>/<id>_idle.png`, used for pickups) and the 128 px icon (`png/128/<id>.png`, used for slot buttons and the Field Guide).
- `items/meat.png` + `items/meat_idle.png` (`meat()`): the Market currency icon (96 px) and pickup loop from `../Assets/Rok_Meat_Currency` (falls back to a drawn placeholder if that's missing). The game loads the icon through `Hud.MEAT_ICON`.
- The same 1024 px icon is the **boot splash** (`project.godot` `application/boot_splash/image`, kept to aspect on a dark jungle green, shown at least `minimum_display_time` 1.2 s), the iOS launch screen's background colour, and the Android launcher (main and adaptive) and splash icon (see the root CLAUDE.md).
- `icon.png` + `assets/icon_1024.png` (`icon()`): the user's app icon `../Assets/Rok_Group_App_Icon_1024.png` (Rok, Nira and Boro; opaque RGB, as iOS requires). The 1024 px copy is the iOS export icon (`export_presets.cfg` `icons/icon_1024x1024`, from which the other iOS sizes are generated) and `icon.png` is it scaled to 256 px, the project icon (`application/config/icon`). Without that file it falls back to Rok's front render. (Without Pillow, `sips -z 256 256` makes the same 256 px icon.)

Rerun it after the source art changes:
`python3 tools/prepare_assets.py`, then `godot --headless --path . --import`.

`prepare_dinos.gd` (a Godot script, no Pillow needed) imports the Dinosaur Expansion from `../Assets/Rok_Dinosaur_Expansion/Rok_Dinosaur_Expansion (2)` (the first 16; it falls back to the idle-only pack), then the 12 species `../Assets/Rok_Balanced_32_Form_Pack` adds (its `new_character_ids`: Deinonychus, Utahraptor, Coelophysis, Oviraptor, Styracosaurus, Centrosaurus, Euoplocephalus, Tapejara, Tupandactylus, Nyctosaurus, Rhamphorhynchus, Dsungaripterus), appended to the catalogue order. That pack's metadata (`characters`, `role`) is normalised to the expansion's shape by `_balanced()` (Flyers become `pterosaurs`; the reference height is the first idle frame's visual height, as in the expansion), and its species use their own painted duck loop (`own_duck`) instead of one built from the slide. The pack's other forms (the originals and the first 16, preserved byte-for-byte) are not re-imported. Per creature it writes the Market card loop (`assets/dinos/<id>_idle.png`, idle frames cropped to their shared bounds and fitted in 160 px), a button portrait (`assets/portraits/<id>.png`), and the playable sheets in `assets/characters/<id>/` (idle, walk, run, jump, slide, duck, die; fly and glide for pterosaurs). Every clip is re-centred on one canvas per species (some clips have extra padding with their own pivot; the game uses one pivot per form) and scaled so the creature stands as tall as its category's original form (Runner 112, Smasher 136, Tank 152, Flyer 124 px), capped at 240 px wide. The slide sheet (enter 0–1, loop 2–5, exit 6–7) is remapped to the game's enter 0–1 / glide 2–4 / recover 5–7, and duck is built from the held slide poses. Pterosaur flight art (registered at the beak tip) is shifted down if needed so it reaches no higher above the feet than the Pteranodon's. `assets/dinos/dinos.json` stores the catalogue text, card frames, and per species the playable frame size, pivot, frame times and measured standing/sliding/flying bounds (`Dinos.form_data` turns them into hitboxes). Run it with `godot --headless --path . -s tools/prepare_dinos.gd`, then `godot --headless --path . --import`.

`prepare_meat.gd` (Godot, no Pillow) imports the meat currency from `../Assets/Rok_Meat_Currency`: `assets/items/meat.png` (the 128 px icon scaled to 96 px) and `assets/items/meat_idle.png` (the 128 px 4×2 pickup loop, 100 ms frames). `prepare_assets.py`'s `meat()` reads the same folder when Pillow is available. Run `godot --headless --path . -s tools/prepare_meat.gd`, then `--import`.

## Sounds
`make_sounds.py` (needs numpy, which isn't installed globally here: run it with `uv run --no-project --with numpy python tools/make_sounds.py`) synthesises every WAV in `assets/sounds/` at 22.05 kHz mono with sine and saw sweeps, filtered noise and simple envelopes. There are no source audio files.
- Effects: jump, double_jump, land, shift, form_rok, form_raptor, form_triceratops, form_spinosaurus, form_pteranodon (pitched roars and screeches), flap, smash, amber, egg, shield, hit, fall, click, denied, start, slide (`slide_sound()`: a downward whoosh as Rok drops, a soft hip thud, a gritty scrape with uneven friction and a few pebble ticks, fading and darkening), intro, unlock, trex_roar (a long bellow), stomp (heavy footstep) and bite (jaw snap).
- Per-form and surface sounds: step_rok, step_raptor, step_heavy, step_grass, step_sand, step_rock, jump_raptor, jump_heavy, land_heavy, and trex_growl.
- **Keep the noise stream stable**: every sound draws from one seeded `rng`, so `slide_sound()` runs last with its own rng (`sfx()` still makes the old slide's two `noise()` draws) and adding it changed no other WAV. Add new sounds the same way, at the end.
- `death_jingle.wav` (`death_jingle()`): an original ~2 s "oh no" jingle with a quick descending marimba run in the game's pentatonic scale, a drooping flute note with a wobble, and two soft drum thumps.
- `music_desert.wav` (92 BPM, E Phrygian dominant, doum-tek hand drums, plucked lead) and `music_volcanic.wav` (84 BPM, dark minor ostinato, heavy low drums, rumbling pad) come from `zone_music()`.
- `music.wav` (the Forest track): a 16-bar loop at 100 BPM in A minor pentatonic (about 38 s) with toms, shaker, woodblock, bass, marimba riff and a flute melody in the second half. The decay tail is folded back to the start so the loop is seamless.

- **Ugo's mumbles** (`mumble_sounds()`, its own rng, written last): `mumble_1..6`, 2–6 voiced caveman syllables each (a gravelly buzz through two vowel formants, a low wobbly pitch that sinks at the end, short gaps, a little breath). `make_sounds.py mumble` writes just these.
- **Break effects** (`break_sounds()`, its own rng, written last): `weapon_break` (a crack, a splintering tear and bits clattering down; a weapon reaching 0 durability), `break_wood` (dry crack, creaking splinters, hollow thump; logs, fences, thorns, vines, crumbling bridges) and `break_rock` (crunch and tumbling rubble; rocks, boulders, walls, meteors). `uv run --no-project --with numpy python tools/make_sounds.py breaks` writes just these three (the full run is slow and leaves every other WAV unchanged).

- **Power-up sounds** (`powerup_sounds()`, its own rng, written last): `powerup_pickup` (four rising bell notes and a glassy shimmer), `powerup_activate` (a rising filtered whoosh into a warm major chord and a sparkle) and `powerup_expire` (a falling three-note chime with a darkening fizzle). `make_sounds.py powerups` writes just these.

Run `python3 tools/make_sounds.py`, then `godot --headless --path . --import`.

## Traps (`traps()`)
Writes `assets/traps/*.png` + `traps/traps.json` from `Rok_Trap_Expansion/trap_manifest.json`. Trap frames are scaled ×7/16 (512 → 224, same scale as Rok). Existing-prop break frames (stone, boulder, fence, log) are scaled ×0.45 so they line up with the props. Liquids (forest_water, desert_quicksand, volcanic_lava) are split into `_left`, `_mid` (repeatable) and `_right` sheets. `forest_overhead` and `desert_fossil_arch` are trimmed and scaled ×0.62. The Spinosaurus swim sheet goes to `characters/spinosaurus/swim.png`. All of these are used in gameplay (see scripts/CLAUDE.md, "Traps by zone").

## Attack clips (`prepare_attacks.py`)
`uv run --no-project --with pillow python tools/prepare_attacks.py` imports the user's `../Assets/Rok_Attack_Animations` (`manifest.json`: 4×2 one-shots, strike/release on frame 4; heroes 384×512 cells, most dinosaurs 512×384): Rok, Nira and Boro × club, axe, spear, bone hammer, sling, torch, bow (named by `Gear.ITEMS` id: `attack_stone_axe`, `attack_flint_spear`, …) and 20 dinosaurs (`pterosaur` → `pteranodon`); the T-rex's sheet is skipped (the pursuer keeps its bite). Each sheet is drawn at its own size and ground line, so it is fitted to the character's in-game idle (`assets/characters/<id>/idle.png`, 1x units before the form's sprite scale): scale = idle frame 0's height over attack frame 0's (alpha > 128 box; checked by eye for all 41 against idle, weapons included), and attack frame 0's feet (centre of the bottom 10%) and bottom land on idle frame 0's, relative to the pivot. All frames share the sideways offset (lunges and tail swings keep their motion); for ground characters each frame's bottom is put back on frame 0's (several strike frames were drawn up to ~25 px higher and floated), flyers (`FLYERS`) keep their drawn rise. Each clip gets its own canvas (union of its frames, so a spear isn't cropped) and pivot. Alpha below 24 is wiped. Writes `assets/attacks/<id>/<clip>.png` + `@2x` and `assets/attacks/attacks.json` (frame, pivot, scale, `times` = `TIMES`, `active`, `release`, `direction`). Run after the character art is final, then `--import`.

## Seamless running attacks (`prepare_running_attacks.py`)

The carry run keeps the run canvas (swapped frame for frame); the attack frames get 64 source px of padding on every side so a swing isn't cropped. Only Rok so far (Nira's and Boro's runs come from `prepare_heroes.gd` with their own placement). Run after `prepare_form_art.py`, then `--import`.

## Rok's voice (`prepare_voice.py`)
Rok's "time to run!" shout (`rok_alarm`) is a **real recording the user supplies**: synthesised voices (a formant "Uh-OH!" yelp) and non-vocal alarms (horn, war drums, cartoon boing) were all tried and rejected, so don't synthesise it again. Drop the clip at `../Assets/Rok_Voice/rok_alarm.<wav|m4a|mp3|aiff|aif|caf>` and run `python3 tools/prepare_voice.py` (standard library plus macOS `afconvert`; `--src`, `--out`, `--gain DB`), then `--import`. It converts to 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit, trims the silence at both ends (keeping 15 ms), adds 5 ms fades and normalises to 90%, writing `assets/sounds/rok_alarm.wav`. Until that file exists `Audio` skips it and the alarm is silent.

Rok: `Rok's Eight-Pose Idle Sprite Sheet-1.png`, `…Walking…-2`, `…Run…-3`, `…Jump…-4`, `Rok's polished eight-frame crouch sheet-5.png` (duck), `Rok's eight-pose sliding sprite sheet-6.png`, `Rok’s Celebration Jump Sprite Sheet-7.png`, `Rok Caveman Defeat Sprite Sheet-8.png` (the death hop). Triceratops: `Eight-frame Triceratops idle sprite sheet-2.png`, `Eight-Frame Triceratops Walk Cycle-9.png` (the pack's 1774×887 idle -1 and walk -3 exports of the same clips are off the game's grid and unused; the walk is drawn a little smaller than its other clips, as in the original pack), `Rok’s Triceratops Charge Sprite Sheet-4.png` (run), `Polished triceratops jump sprite sheet-5.png`, `Triceratops Crouch Sprite Sheet-6.png`, `Rok's Triceratops slide animation sheet-7.png`, `Rok's Triceratops hit-and-defeat sprites-8.png` (death hop). Pteranodon: the pterosaur pack (`Rok's pterosaur idle sprite sheet-1.png`, `…Walk…-2`, `…Run…-3`, `…Jump…-4`, `Rok's crouching pterosaur sprite sheet-5.png`, `Rok’s pterosaur slide sprite sheet-6.png`, `Rok’s Pterosaur Takeoff Animation-8.png`, `Rok’s Eight-Pose Pterosaur Flight Loop-9.png`, `Rok’s Pterosaur Glide Sprite Sheet-10.png`, `Rok's Pterosaur Landing Sprite Sheet-11.png`, `Rok’s Pterosaur Defeat Sprite Sheet-7.png`) is on 384×512 frames (`new_frame`; the original Pteranodon's are 512×512) and drawn ~17% smaller, so each frame is scaled ×1.2 (`scale`) before it's placed on the 512×512 canvas; its jump sheet's haze reaches alpha ~63, so that sheet is cleared below 72 (`clear`). Its flight frames follow the original's pose for pose, so per-frame alignment keeps the original flight motion. The pack's second takeoff (`Pterosaur takeoff sprite sheet-12.png`) draws it smaller and is unused. Spinosaurus: `Wide eight-frame Spinosaurus idle sheet-2.png` (the 1774×887 idle -1 is unused), `Polished Spinosaurus Walk Cycle Sprite Sheet-3.png`, `Polished Spinosaurus Run Cycle Sheet-4.png`, `Polished Spinosaurus jump sprite sheet-5.png`, `Crouching Spinosaurus eight-pose sprite sheet-6.png`, `Rok’s Spinosaurus slide transformation sheet-7.png`, `Rok's Spinosaurus Swim Sprite Sheet-9.png` (swim, aligned to the trap expansion's old swim so it floats at the same waterline), `Rok's Spinosaurus Hit-Defeat Sprite Sheet-8.png` (death hop). Raptor: `Rok's Feathered Raptor Idle Frames-1.png`, `Rok's Feathered Raptor Walk Cycle-2.png`, `Rok’s eight-frame feathered raptor run-3.png`, `Orange raptor jump sprite sheet-4.png`, `Rok's crouching raptor sprite sheet-5.png`, `Feathered Raptor Slide Sprite Sheet-6.png`, `Feathered raptor defeat sprite sheet-7.png`. `uv run --no-project --with pillow python tools/prepare_form_art.py` writes every file the game draws for each form at 1x (7/16) **and** a `<name>@2x.png` twin (7/8, exactly twice the pixels): `characters/<form>/{idle,walk,run,jump,duck,slide,die}` (+ `celebrate` for Rok), `portraits/<form>` and `reactions/<form>/{trip,dizzy,snag}` (no new reaction art yet: the hit-reaction pack's sheets). The new sheets don't keep one ground line (Rok's run sheet's second row sits 32 px higher, each sheet has its own height), so every frame is moved so its art's bottom and centre (alpha > 128 bounds) land where the original pack's same frame had them; frame sizes, pivots and hitboxes are unchanged (the art is the same size as the old, within about 6%). Alpha below 24 is wiped (the Raptor's crouch and the Triceratops's walk carried a faint haze, alpha ≤ 8, over their whole canvas). The death hop is packed like `prepare_assets.py`'s `deaths()` and rewrites that form's entry in `characters/die.json`. A missing new sheet falls back to the original pack. To add another form, add a `FORMS` entry. **Run it after `prepare_assets.py`, `prepare_reactions.gd` and `prepare_animation_pack.gd`**, which write the old art over these files. Then `--import`.

The game loads character and portrait art through `SpriteLib.tex(path)`, which prefers the `@2x` twin and overrides its size to the 1x size, so all frame and pivot numbers stay in 1x units and only the sampling gets sharper (high-DPI phones). The 1x files stay as the fallback and for code that reads pixels (`summary.gd` icons). Another character gets the same treatment by writing its own `@2x` twins.

## Gear art and sockets (`prepare_gear.py`)

It also reads the user's `../Assets/Rok_Weapons_Animation_Pack` (512 px frames; `weapons_pack()`): the **Torch** (`sprites/torch_lit.png` icon + `spritesheets/torch_burning.png` → `gear/torch_anim.png`, registered to the icon by an `origin`) and the **Bow** (`sprites/bow.png`), both `upright`, the **arrow** (`gear/arrow.png`) and a **break effect** per older weapon (`spritesheets/<weapon>_break.png` → `gear/break_<id>.png`, scaled so frame 0's intact weapon matches the icon; `gear_art.json` `break` holds frame size, origin and the pack's 12 FPS). ## Seamless terrain (`prepare_terrain.py`)
`uv run --no-project --with pillow python tools/prepare_terrain.py` imports the user's `../Assets/Rok_Seamless_Terrain` (the terrain set rebuilt to join seamlessly: `standard/` at the game's sizes, `hd/` at 4× for grass pieces, dirt and cobble or 2× for the 1280×420 strips) into `assets/terrain/<name>.png` + an `@2x` twin from the HD art: mid_a/b, cap_left/right, slope_up/down, drop, dirt, cobble, and the desert, volcanic and **forest** ground strips. `terrain.json` is rebuilt from the pack's join rows (`tiles.json` connectors; flats join at y 8, slopes' low ends at 61): each piece's `left`/`right` grass line is its join row − 3 (`JOIN_TO_TIP`, so the tips keep `FEET_BELOW_GRASS`'s meaning) and **one level is the slope's rise, 53 px** (it was 47: hills and drops are ~12% taller; physics reads it from the JSON). The drop falls only 48, so it's scaled ×53/48 (80×93) to fall one level; that costs its pixel-exact joins (a faint seam where it meets the flat on its left, ≤ 1 px at the top). `mid` and the physics `profile` are measured as before. The Forest now uses the seamless `forest_ground` strip (`Zones` forest `ground: "forest"`, built by `Terrain._build_strip` like the Desert's and Volcanic's), because the tiled flats showed a seam where their bottoms met the dirt fill and `mid_b` is `mid_a` mirrored (a repeating butterfly pattern); the tiles still exist (the Market scene uses mid_a + dirt) and `"tiles"` brings them back. Then `--import`.
