# Rok Runner

A prehistoric runner game: Rok flees a hunter, shapeshifts into dinosaurs and
grows stronger between runs. These are the words the game and its docs use.

## Modes

**Endless Run**:
The open-ended run: it goes on until Rok is caught or runs out of hearts, and it's where the player grows (meat, materials, XP, best distance); it never gives Boss Materials or eggs.
_Avoid_: Normal mode, free run

**Campaign**:
The mode of set challenges the player clears one by one, made of Chapters: where the player proves the growth made in Endless Runs and earns what meat can't buy (Boss Materials, eggs).
_Avoid_: Story mode, adventure

**Chapter**:
A group of Stages in the Campaign.
_Avoid_: World, act

**Stage**:
One Campaign challenge.
_Avoid_: Level (that word is the character level), mission (that's a daily or weekly goal)

**Introduction**:
The first time the player meets a hazard, in an Endless Run or a Stage: a NEW banner and the hazard alone at first. Once met in either mode it counts in both.
_Avoid_: Unlock (unlocks are for dinosaurs and items), tutorial (that's the first-run lesson)

**Trail**:
A short side path of Stages about one species, in its Home Zone, that pays one random reward from its pool each time it's cleared, until the pool is empty.
_Avoid_: Side quest, species chapter

**Home Zone**:
The zone a species belongs to: where it runs wild and where its Trail is.
_Avoid_: Habitat, biome (biome obstacles are a zone's look)

**Puzzle Stage**:
A Stage built from a fixed list of puzzle pieces, slower and with no Hunter, ending at a finish line.
_Avoid_: Puzzle level, brain stage

**Checkpoint**:
A totem in a Puzzle Stage where Rok restarts after a mistake.
_Avoid_: Save point, respawn

**Switch**:
A stone in a Puzzle Stage that Rok hits with ATTACK to change the course (lower a bridge, open a gate).
_Avoid_: Lever, button

**Gem**:
A coloured key in a Puzzle Stage that opens the Door of the same colour.
_Avoid_: Key, crystal (a Time Crystal is a power-up)

**Door**:
A coloured stone barrier in a Puzzle Stage, open only with its Gem.
_Avoid_: Gate (a dino gate needs a dinosaur, not a Gem)

**Star**:
One of a Stage's three goals, kept for good once met: the first is clearing the Stage, the other two are set per Stage.
_Avoid_: Medal (medals are badge tiers), rating

**Torch**:
What a failed Stage costs: the player holds a few, and they relight over time.
_Avoid_: Energy (that's PRIMAL energy in a run), lives (those are hearts), stamina, tickets

## Dinosaurs in a run

**Hunter**:
The big dinosaur that chases Rok from behind (the T-rex in the Forest, the Carnotaurus in the Desert, the Allosaurus in the Volcanic Badlands); if it catches him, the run ends.
_Avoid_: Chaser, pursuer

**Boss**:
The dinosaur Rok fights in a boss stage: it runs ahead of him and he wears down its health with ATTACK. Any species can be a Boss, big or small; a hunter species used this way is called the Boss in that stage.
_Avoid_: Hunter (when it's the one being fought), enemy

## Gear

**Accessory**:
A hero's third gear piece, giving one special effect rather than attribute points.
_Avoid_: Trinket, charm (a protection charm is something else)

**Upgrade**:
A gear piece's +1 to +10 strength steps, bought at Kora's bench; from +7 an upgrade can fail.
_Avoid_: Enhance, level (Level is a character's rank)

**Worn Out**:
A gear piece at 0 durability: it still works, at half its bonus, until repaired.
_Avoid_: Broken (a piece only breaks on a failed Upgrade, and is then gone)

**Boss Material**:
What a Boss drops, used to craft its Boss Set.
_Avoid_: Trophy (trophies are the hunters' charge drops), loot

**Boss Set**:
A Boss's three matching gear pieces (weapon, armor, accessory); wearing all three gives a set bonus.
_Avoid_: Kit, outfit

**Protection Charm**:
A rare item ("Kora's Blessing") that stops a failed Upgrade from dropping or breaking the piece.
_Avoid_: Insurance, catalyst (catalysts change crafting odds)

## Growth

**Level**:
A hero's or dinosaur's rank, 1 to 20, gained automatically when it has the XP.
_Avoid_: Using "level" for a Stage

**Strength**:
One number for how strong the player's team is: the hero (Level, skills, gear) plus the four equipped dinosaurs; each Stage recommends one.
_Avoid_: Power (that's an attribute), rating, CP

**Milestone**:
A set Level (3, 5, 8, 13, 15, 18) at which a dinosaur gains something that changes only its own form: a Trick or a Trait. New looks come only with Evolution.
_Avoid_: Reward tier, unlock

**Trick**:
Something new a dinosaur's form can do, learned at a Milestone (for example the Raptor's Double Dash). An expansion dinosaur shares its category's Tricks except one Signature Trick of its own.
_Avoid_: Move, ability, skill (Skills are a hero's)

**Trait**:
An always-on bonus to a dinosaur's own form, gained at a Milestone (for example the Raptor's Quick Start).
_Avoid_: Perk (perks come from badges and attribute thresholds), passive

**Set Bonus**:
The one extra effect a full Boss Set gives, working in every form.
_Avoid_: Set perk, combo

**Evolution**:
A runner dinosaur's change to a new look and a jump in strength at Levels 10 and 20.
_Avoid_: Ascension, prestige, growing (pets grow by age)

**Skill Tree**:
A hero's own set of skills, bought with the Skill Points that hero earns by levelling; it can be reset for free.
_Avoid_: Talents, perks (perks come from badges)

**Mission**:
A daily or weekly goal that pays meat, behind the Missions button.
_Avoid_: Quest, task, stage
