# Rok Runner

A prehistoric runner game: Rok flees a hunter, shapeshifts into dinosaurs and
grows stronger between runs. These are the words the game and its docs use.

## Modes

**Endless Run**:
The open-ended run: it goes on until Rok is caught or runs out of hearts, and it's where the player grows (meat, materials, XP, best distance).
_Avoid_: Normal mode, free run

**Campaign**:
The mode of set challenges the player clears one by one, made of Chapters.
_Avoid_: Story mode, adventure

**Chapter**:
A group of Stages in the Campaign.
_Avoid_: World, act

**Stage**:
One Campaign challenge.
_Avoid_: Level (that word is the character level), mission (that's a daily or weekly goal)

## Growth

**Level**:
A hero's or dinosaur's rank, 1 to 10, raised at the Camp with XP and meat.
_Avoid_: Using "level" for a Stage

**Mission**:
A daily or weekly goal that pays meat, behind the Missions button.
_Avoid_: Quest, task, stage
