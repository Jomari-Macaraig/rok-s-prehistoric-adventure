# What stages pay

## Question

What does a first clear pay (meat, materials, cards, XP, energy or tickets), what does a replay pay, and what do stars pay (per star, or at star totals per chapter)? How do campaign rewards compare to endless-run earnings, so both are worth playing?

## Resolution

Decided with the user (2026-11-24); all amounts in config, growing with
each chapter:
- **Normal stage, first clear**: XP for the hero and every dinosaur used, a
  handful of the zone's materials, a little meat, a chance of a card; every
  few stages (for example every 3rd) also **a torch**.
- **Replays**: about a fifth of the first clear's XP and meat, a smaller
  chance of materials; no cards or torches.
- **Stars**: each star's first earning pays a small bonus (XP or
  materials); a chapter's star totals (for example 10, 20, 30) each open a
  **bonus chest** (rare materials, a Kora's Blessing, torches).
- **Boss stages**: first clear **3 boss materials** guaranteed plus big XP;
  a replay gives **1 boss material with a 40% chance** (a set takes real
  effort).
- **Puzzle stages**: first clear gives a rare material and a good chance of
  a Kora's Blessing.
- **Trails**: one random reward from the Trail's pool, each once (see "How
  the campaign grows with more species").
- **Results screen**: rewards appear one by one (stars, XP bars filling,
  items dropping in), with "NEW!" on first-clear and star rewards.

Feeds: the screens (results), balance (checking earnings against endless
runs), and the stage settings (each stage's reward amounts).
