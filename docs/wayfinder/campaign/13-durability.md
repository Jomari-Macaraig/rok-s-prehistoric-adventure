# Durability and repair: keep, change or drop

## Question

Is wear and repair fun or a chore? Today weapons wear 1 per ATTACK that connects and armor 1 per hit taken, broken gear gives nothing until repaired for materials (Camp or Items screen). Keep it as is, soften it (no breaking, only lower bonuses), tie it to the campaign (stages wear gear), or drop it? What should repair cost, and does selling stay tied to durability left?

## Resolution

Decided with the user (2026-11-24):
- **Soften, don't drop**: gear never stops working. At 0 durability a piece
  is **worn out** and gives **half** its bonus until repaired (no more
  "broken, gives nothing"). Breaking only happens on a failed risky upgrade
  (see "Weapons, armor and accessories: what gear is for").
- **Wear per run**: each run or stage a piece is worn in uses **1
  durability** (shown as "12 runs left"); no more per-hit wear.
- **Repair costs meat**, more for rarer and higher-upgraded pieces (in
  config), at the Camp and on Kora's Items screen.
- **A successful upgrade restores full durability** (a failed one doesn't).
- **Selling** still scales with durability left; the upgrade level raises a
  piece's value.

Feeds: "What the Market is for" (meat sinks: repairs), "How recommended
strength is worked out" (worn-out pieces count at half), building (repair
and wear rework; old saves' broken gear becomes worn out).
