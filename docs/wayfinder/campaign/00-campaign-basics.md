# Campaign basics

## Question

What is a stage, how is the campaign laid out, and how does it cycle with endless runs?

## Resolution

Decided while charting (2026-11-24):
- A **stage** is a run of the same game (same controls, hazards, forms) that ends at a **finish line** after a set distance. Some stages will be **boss stages** (a boss dinosaur) and some **puzzle-like** (see their own tickets).
- **Layout**: one chapter per zone (Forest, Desert, Canyon, Volcanic), about 10 stages each, a boss stage at the end of each. Later the campaign grows to include **every dinosaur species**, so there will be many stages (see "How the campaign grows with more species").
- **Strength**: each stage shows a **recommended strength**; you can still try any stage you've reached.
- **Unlocking**: the campaign opens at a certain best distance **and** level (see "When the campaign opens"); each stage opens when the one before is cleared.
- **Cost**: playing a stage costs something (energy, tickets or similar), to be tied to ads later (see "What a stage costs to play").
- **Rewards**: a big first-clear reward, a small one on replays.
- **Stars**: 3 per stage (finish, plus two extra goals), with bonus rewards.
- **Courses** come from config settings (length, hazards, difficulty, zone), generated by the game, not hand-placed.
- Pets joining stages is wanted later (out of scope for this map).
