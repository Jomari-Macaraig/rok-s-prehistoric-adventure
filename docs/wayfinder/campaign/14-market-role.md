# What the Market is for

## Question

What should the Market sell and buy in the new loop? Today it sells power-ups, booster slot, heart containers, expansion dinosaurs, heroes and basic material bundles, and buys the player's gear, materials and spare cards at 80% (docs/systems/marketplace.md). Should it sell gear or accessories, torches, level boosts? What's the role of meat versus campaign rewards, so endless runs and the campaign both stay worth playing?

## Resolution

Decided with the user (2026-11-24):
- **Stays on sale**: power-ups, the 3rd booster slot, heart containers,
  the expansion dinosaurs (with badge tiers), heroes, basic material
  bundles.
- **New**: the **torch refill** (about 150 meat, also on the campaign
  screen) and **Kora's Blessing** (a protection charm), pricey and limited
  to a few a week.
- **Not sold**: gear, XP boosts, eggs, boss materials.
- **Today's deals**: 2-3 offers that change daily (a discounted dinosaur, a
  rare material bundle, a cheap Blessing).
- **Who earns what**: **endless runs** are the main source of **meat** (and
  common materials); **the campaign** pays what meat can't buy (boss
  materials, cards, XP, eggs, torches, some rare materials) and only a
  little meat.
- **Selling** stays at 80% for gear, materials and spare cards, and adds
  **boss materials**; **eggs can't be sold**.
- **Real money** (a premium currency, purchases, ads) is out of scope; the
  Market just leaves room for it.

Feeds: "What stages pay" (campaign pays non-meat rewards), the screens
(deals shelf, torch refill, Blessing).
