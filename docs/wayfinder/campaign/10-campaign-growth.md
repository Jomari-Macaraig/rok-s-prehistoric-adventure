# How the campaign grows with more species

## Question

The campaign will eventually include every dinosaur species (more are coming). How do new species turn into stages and chapters: a chapter per zone with stages featuring its species, chapters per species group, new zones, or side chapters? Does clearing a species' stages give something for that species (its card, its unlock)?

## Resolution

Decided with the user (2026-11-24):
- **Shape**: the zone chapters are the **main path** (new zones become new
  chapters after the Volcanic Badlands), plus a short **Trail** per species
  as side content.
- **Trail**: about 3 stages per species: meet it running wild; a stage
  built around its strength (Runner race, Smasher wall-breaking, Tank water
  crossing, Flyer sky trail); beat it as a boss.
- **Home zone**: every species (all 32 and new ones) gets a home zone in
  config: where it can run wild, and the zone its Trail uses.
- **Trail opens** once the home zone's chapter is cleared (its boss beaten).
- **Trail reward**: a **reward pool** (its card, a chunk of its XP if owned,
  a Market discount (about 30%) if not owned, and an **egg**). Clearing the
  Trail's last stage gives **one at random**; replaying it rolls again, and
  each pool reward is won **only once**, so retries work through the pool;
  once it's empty a replay gives the small normal replay reward.
- **Eggs are in from the start**: the pet system comes right after the
  campaign (the user), so eggs wait in the player's items until then.
- **Time and weather per stage**: each stage sets its time (day, dusk,
  night) and weather (sunny, cloudy, rain; plus storm, fog, eclipse). Night
  is darker (hazards harder to spot); rain adds puddles and slides go a bit
  further; sunny and cloudy are looks only.
- **Endless runs** get the same: rain and clouds come and go at random with
  the same effects.
- **New zones** join both the campaign (a new chapter) and the endless-run
  zone cycle.

Feeds: "Loading each stage" (a stage pulls in one zone, its boss and its
species), "What stages pay" (Trail pools, eggs), the stage settings (time,
weather, home zones), and the pet system (eggs).
