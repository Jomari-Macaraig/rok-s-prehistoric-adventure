# The list of star goals

## Question

Each stage has 3 stars: finishing, plus two extra goals. Which goal types exist (collect N amber or meat, take no damage, finish within a time, smash N obstacles, never stumble, finish as a certain dinosaur...)? Do stars stay earned once earned, and can they be earned over several attempts?

## Resolution

Decided with the user (2026-11-24):
- **Star 1** is always "Clear the stage" (reach the finish line, beat the
  boss, or finish the puzzle). Each stage's config picks its **two other
  goals** from the list below, with their numbers.
- **The goal list**:
  - collecting: collect N amber; collect N meat; find the hidden fossil;
  - skill: take no damage; finish with at least N hearts; reach a xN
    combo; never stumble;
  - fighting: smash N obstacles; bonk N wild dinosaurs;
  - dinosaurs: finish without shapeshifting; run N m as a given dinosaur;
  - boss stages only: beat the boss with at least N hearts; beat it before
    half its escape distance;
  - puzzle stages only: no checkpoint returns; collect every gem (hidden
    ones too); no hints.
  - No time limits (Rok sets his own pace).
- **Stars add up across tries** and stay earned for good.
- **Shown**: the briefing lists the 3 goals; a small tracker shows progress
  in the run, and a star pops with a sound when a goal is met.
- **Stars never lock anything**: clearing a chapter's boss opens the next
  chapter; star totals per chapter open **bonus chests** (their contents in
  "What stages pay").

Feeds: "What settings define a stage" (two goals and their numbers per
stage), "What stages pay" (star chests), the screens (briefing, tracker,
results).
