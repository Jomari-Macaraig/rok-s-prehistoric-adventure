# What happens when a stage fails

## Question

When Rok runs out of hearts, the boss escapes, or the player quits: what does the screen show (what went wrong, progress kept such as stars already earned), what are the choices (retry now, back to the chapter, go do an Endless Run), the torch that's spent, and is there a nudge when the player keeps failing ("Recommended strength 40, you have 28: try leveling up in Endless Runs")?

## Resolution

Decided with the user (2026-11-24):
- **The fail screen says** what happened ("Caught by the T-rex", "Out of
  hearts", "The Allosaurus escaped", "You left the stage"), how far Rok got
  ("820 / 1,200 m" or "Boss health left: 35%") and the torch spent ("🔥 1
  spent (2 left)").
- **Kept on a failure**: XP from metres run and the meat and materials
  picked up. **Not given**: first-clear rewards.
- **Stars only on a clear** (no star goals earned on a failed try).
- **Choices**: Retry (instant), Back to the chapter, Endless Run. Out of
  torches, Retry becomes "Refill for 150 meat" with the next-torch
  countdown.
- **A nudge after 3 failures in a row** on the same stage: a tip from the
  player's weakest part of Strength (level up a dinosaur in an Endless Run,
  upgrade a weapon at Kora's, bring the suggested dinosaur).

Feeds: the results popup in "The campaign screens", building.
